Auschwitz-Birkenau

Auschwitz I & Auschwitz II-Birkenau

must visit

The former Nazi concentration and extermination camp. A UNESCO World Heritage Site and a powerful memorial to the victims of the Holocaust.

Entry is free but advance reservation required. Tours ~3.5 hrs. Distance: 70 km (~1h 15min) from Kraków.

Więźniów Oświęcimia 20, Oświęcim

Zakopane — Winter Capital

day trip

Poland's most popular mountain resort at the foot of the Tatra Mountains. Wooden architecture, oscypek cheese, and stunning alpine scenery.
