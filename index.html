Wieliczka Salt Mine

Wieliczka Salt Mine

UNESCO

One of the world's oldest salt mines, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Stunning underground chambers, chapels carved in salt, and crystal-clear brine lakes.

Entry requires a guided tour (~3 hrs). Distance: 15 km (~30 min) from Kraków. Temperature underground: ~14°C year-round — bring a jacket!

Daniłowicza 10, Wieliczka
